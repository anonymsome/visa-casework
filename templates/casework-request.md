# Template: casework request

The main written request. Goes in the form's "describe your problem" field,
or attached as a letter where the office accepts one.

**Every bracket is a slot for a fact the user gave in intake. A slot with no
fact behind it gets deleted, not filled with something plausible.**

Target length: under one page. Caseworkers read fast.

---

Dear Representative [MEMBER LAST NAME],

I am a constituent living in [CITY, STATE] in [DISTRICT]. I am writing to
ask your office to inquire with the Department of State about consular
appointment availability that is preventing me from traveling.

I have held [STATUS CATEGORY] status since [YEAR], and I currently
[WORK AT / STUDY AT] [EMPLOYER OR SCHOOL] in [CITY]. My status in the
United States is valid and unexpired. My visa stamp, however, expired on
[DATE], which means that if I leave the country I cannot return without a
new one.

[THE BLOCKAGE — which post, what the wait is, what has been tried. Concrete
and checkable. For example: the earliest available appointment at the post
in CITY is DATE, roughly N months out; the user has checked the portal
regularly since DATE; third-country posts at X and Y show no availability
either. If an expedite was already requested, say when and what came back.]

[WHAT IS ON THE OTHER SIDE — the reason travel matters. Names, dates,
specifics. A parent's illness and when it began; a funeral that was missed;
a dependent abroad and how long the separation has run. Use the user's own
words. If the situation is serious inconvenience rather than hardship, say
that accurately — an honest, modest statement is filable and a dressed-up
one is not.]

I understand that your office cannot approve a visa or direct a consular
officer's decision, and I am not asking for that. I am asking whether your
office can inquire with the Department of State about appointment
availability at [POST], and whether my circumstances warrant expedited
consideration.

I am glad to sign a Privacy Act release and to provide any documentation
that would help. I can be reached at [PHONE] or [EMAIL].

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
[FULL NAME]
[STREET ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE, ZIP]

---

## Checks before showing this to the user

- No passport number, visa control number, A-number, receipt number, or
  full date of birth anywhere in the text.
- The ask is inquiry and expedite *consideration*. The words "approve,"
  "issue," and "grant" do not appear as things the office should do.
- Every remaining sentence traces to something the user actually said.
- Home address is present — it is how the office verifies district
  residency, and it is the one address that has to be there.
- It fits on a page.
