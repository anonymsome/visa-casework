# The Privacy Act release, in plain words

## What it is

Federal agencies cannot discuss a person's file with a third party without
that person's written authorization. 5 U.S.C. § 552a. A congressional
office asking State about someone's visa case is a third party. So every
casework form carries an authorization — a box, a signature line, or a
separate form — and without it the office literally cannot proceed.

## What signing it does

Authorizes the member's office to obtain and discuss the user's records with
the relevant agency, for this inquiry. It is narrow: a specific office, a
specific matter. It is not a general waiver, not a grant of representation,
and not a power of attorney.

## Why the agent never signs it

This is the firmest rule in the plugin, and it holds even when the user says
to go ahead.

The signature is an attestation by a specific person that the statements are
theirs and true, and that they authorize release of their own records. An
agent checking that box produces an authorization no one actually made, on a
federal form, about federal records. If it is ever questioned, the user owns
a false attestation they did not knowingly make.

So: Browser MCP fills the factual fields and stops. The consent box, the
signature, and submit are the user's, every time. If the user asks the agent
to just click it, decline in one sentence and explain why once.

## What to tell the user

- They are authorizing an inquiry about their own records. Normal, and
  required.
- It is scoped to this office and this matter.
- They should read the text on the actual form — wording varies by office —
  rather than relying on this summary.
- If the authorization covers anyone else (a spouse, a dependent whose case
  is entangled with theirs), that person signs their own.

## Identifiers

The form asks for identifiers the drafts deliberately omit: passport number,
visa control number, A-number, case or receipt numbers, full date of birth.
That is correct — they belong on the form, entered by the user, over the
office's own connection.

They do not belong in the draft files, the tracking file, or the chat. Say
this during intake, not after the user has already pasted a passport number.
